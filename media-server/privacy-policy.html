Privacy Policy

Muskellunge Lake Resort app · Effective July 7, 2026

This app (the “App”) is a private, invite-only community app for members and family of Muskellunge Lake Resort (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information we collect

The App is account-based, and the information below is linked to your account:

  • Account & contact information you provide: name, email address, and optionally phone number, mailing address, household, birthday, bio, and profile photo.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, chat messages, help requests, event RSVPs, cabin booking requests, work-checklist items, and any photos, videos, or files you attach.
  • Optional payment handles: if you choose to add them, your Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or PayPal usernames so other members can reimburse you. These are usernames only — we do not collect or process card or bank-account numbers.
  • Notification identifiers: a device push token, so we can send the notifications you enable.
  • Sign-in records: the date, email, and IP address of sign-ins, kept for account security.

How we use information

  • To operate the App’s features (directory, feed, chat, events, help requests, cabins, and Family Fest).
  • To sign you in via a one-time email code and keep your account secure.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.

We use your information only to provide the App. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How information is stored and shared

Account and content data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Uploaded photos, videos, and files are stored on a private server operated by Muskellunge Lake Resort. Content you post (such as your directory entry, feed posts, and chat messages) is visible to other signed-in members of the community, as you would expect. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

The App uses Apple’s WeatherKit to show local forecasts (using a fixed resort location, not your device location) and Apple Push Notification service to deliver notifications.

Location

The App does not collect your device location.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request that we correct or delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Deleting your account removes your profile and personal information; content already shared with the community may be disassociated from your identity.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for members of the Muskellunge Lake Resort family community and is not directed to children under 13. Family members under the age of 13 should use the App only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details (including optional phone, address, birthday, and payment handles) at any time in the App.
  • Hide yourself from the member directory in your profile settings.
  • Turn notifications on or off in the App and in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@muskellungelakeresort.com
